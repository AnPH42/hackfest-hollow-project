How the proximity demo works
  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge from http://localhost (desktop or Android).
  2. Near the sensor ESP32, click Pair a board and select Hollow-Sensor. Near the receiver ESP32, pair Hollow-Mailbox.
  3. Walk near Hollow-Sensor to pick up an alert, walk out of range over to Hollow-Mailbox to drop it off, then let a second device read it from the mailbox. Don’t reload this tab.
